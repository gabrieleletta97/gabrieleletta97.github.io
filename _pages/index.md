## About

I obtained my **PhD in Economics and Finance** at **Università Cattolica del Sacro Cuore — DEFAP School** in February 2026, supervised by [Gilberto Turati](https://docenti.unicatt.it/ppd2/it/docenti/12695/gilberto-turati) and [Luca Salmasi](https://sites.google.com/view/luca-salmasi/home-page).

In 2025 I was a visiting researcher at **IEB — Universitat de Barcelona**, hosted by [Judit Vall Castelló](https://www.juditvall.com/). I was born and raised in Rome and earned my MSc at Sapienza University of Rome in 2021.
