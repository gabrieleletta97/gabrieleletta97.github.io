<header class="section-banner workshop-banner"><h1>Workshop</h1></header>

<section class="content-box">
  <p>In 2023, I contributed to the launch of the <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/milanphdeconworkshop/home">Milan PhD Economic Workshop</a>, an annual academic event gathering PhD students from the universities in Milan and its surroundings to present and discuss their research in economics.</p>

  <p>Since then, I served as an organizing committee member in the 1st (<a href="https://sites.google.com/view/milanphdeconworkshop/past-events/2023-workshop-unimi">2023</a>), 2nd (<a href="https://sites.google.com/view/milanphdeconworkshop/past-events/2024-workshop-unimib">2024</a>) and 3rd editions (<a href="https://sites.google.com/view/milanphdeconworkshop/home">2025</a>), and as part of the scientific committee in the 4th edition (<a href="https://www.unibocconi.it/en/programs/phd/phd-economics-and-finance/4k-milan-phd-economics-workshop">2026</a>).</p>
</section>
